## Proposed resolution
PREREQ: strictly after `27c560179112` BACKPORT: bpf, xdp: Maintain attached XDP programs in net_device
(this commit adds the `link` member to `struct bpf_xdp_entity` and calls `dev_xdp_attach()`), and after
the `bpf: add bpf_link` / `bpf_link_*` commits that create `struct bpf_link`, `bpf_link_primer`,
`enum bpf_link_type` and `BPF_LINK_CREATE` (none of which exist in sdm670 yet).

Once those are in, resolve as **MERGE**:
- Block 1: take theirs (`bpf_prog_offload_ops`, `bpf_xdp_link`, `enum bpf_xdp_mode`,
  `bpf_xdp_entity` with `link`, `struct netdev_bpf`). Dropping sdm670's `struct netdev_xdp` is safe here:
  its only implementers in a30605a54f3b are mellanox mlx4/mlx5 (`drivers/net/ethernet/mellanox/mlx4/
  en_netdev.c`, `drivers/net/ethernet/mellanox/mlx5/core/en_main.c`) and both are unbuilt
  (`# CONFIG_MLX4_CORE is not set` / `# CONFIG_MLX5_CORE is not set`,
  arch/arm64/configs/gts4lv_eur_open_defconfig:1787-1788 @ a30605a54f3b).
- Block 2: take theirs, and **keep** Samsung's `dev_hard_start_xmit_list()` prototype - unrelated
  adjacent declaration, defined in net/core/dev.c, dropping it breaks the build.
- Blocks 3-9 (bpf.h, uapi/bpf.h, syscall.c): take theirs; these are pure additions of code that does not
  exist in sdm670, the conflict is only the missing surrounding context. Pay attention to the
  `!CONFIG_BPF_SYSCALL` stubs (block 4) - they are easy to drop by accident because the "ours" side is
  empty.
- Blocks 10-11 (net/core/dev.c): take theirs, they depend on the `27c560179112` rework of
  `dev_change_xdp_fd()`.
- Blocks 12-16 (tools/include/uapi/linux/bpf.h): take theirs and keep byte-identical to
  include/uapi/linux/bpf.h, otherwise the userspace `bpftool` build breaks.

Warning for the lead: this is the commit that adds `BPF_LINK_CREATE`. `bpfloader` on Android 16 uses
BPF link for `cgroup/btf` and for `tc`; dropping it silently loses `bpf_link_create()` support even
though the build may pass.
