## Proposed resolution
MERGE. The two lists differ in two independent ways, keep both differences:
```
export ARCH SRCARCH CONFIG_SHELL HOSTCC HOSTCFLAGS CROSS_COMPILE AS LD CC
export CPP AR NM STRIP OBJCOPY OBJDUMP READELF PAHOLE
```
i.e. keep sdm670's `AS` (needed: `AS = $(CROSS_COMPILE)as` at Makefile:364 of sdm670 and the ARM build uses it), and add `READELF PAHOLE` to the second export. Keep the auto-merged `PAHOLE = pahole` and the auto-merged `config DEBUG_INFO_BTF` in lib/Kconfig.debug unchanged.
Dependency note for the lead: this commit is the prerequisite for the later Makefile hook - at the series head `$(PAHOLE)` is consumed by scripts/link-vmlinux.sh:208-224 and scripts/Makefile.modpost:139, and the head's export line also carries `RESOLVE_BTFIDS`, added by `e42fe4677e9c` (also in batch K2a-3).
Caveat: the actual vmlinux BTF link rule is deliberately NOT in this commit (see Mathias Gluszczynski's note in the commit message), so `CONFIG_DEBUG_INFO_BTF=y` alone will not produce BTF here. Whether it is wanted at all depends on the follow-on sysfs-BTF commit.
