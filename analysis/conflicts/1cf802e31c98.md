So bit 0 is deliberately shared by `SKB_DST_NOREF` and `BPF_F_INGRESS`, bit 1 is `BPF_F_STRPARSER` - sdm670 needs no adaptation.
## Proposed resolution
PREREQ, then MERGE.
- PREREQ: `993f49608c23` "bpf, sockmap: convert to generic sk_msg interface" (series pos 953, this commit is pos 1708) creates include/linux/skmsg.h, net/core/skmsg.c and net/core/sock_map.c. sdm670 has none of the three. Without it the three modify/delete conflicts cannot be resolved ("keep theirs" would drop the whole sockmap stack).
- MERGE for include/linux/skbuff.h: take *theirs* verbatim, the anonymous `union` around `_skb_refdst` / `_sk_redir`. sdm670's blank line and plain `unsigned long _skb_refdst;` are dropped, nothing else in that file changes.
- Keep the auto-merged include/net/tcp.h deletion (do not re-add the `bpf` member of `struct tcp_skb_cb`), otherwise `TCP_SKB_CB(skb)->bpf.*` would still be referenced and the two storage schemes would overlap in `skb->cb`.
- Safety note for the reviewer: because `_sk_redir` overlays `_skb_refdst`, every place that starts writing redirect state must drop the dst first. This commit already adds `skb_dst_drop(skb)` in `sk_psock_strp_read` (net/core/skmsg.c) and in both `bpf_sk_redirect_map` / `bpf_sk_redirect_hash` (net/core/sock_map.c) - do not lose those two lines when resolving skmsg.c / sock_map.c, they are the reason the series does not leak dst references.
