context needed. Verified merge output at tree `4d6fba4d4a2d87208e2583e38d480fd7858588b6`:

user-address validation itself, and keeping both is harmless but pointless. Do not drop it: this is the helper
Android's `bpfloader`/`netbpfload` use for user-space writes.
