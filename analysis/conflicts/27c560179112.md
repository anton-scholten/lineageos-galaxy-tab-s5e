## Proposed resolution
PREREQ: must land after the XDP-API upgrade chain, in particular after
`fd90efeb991d` BACKPORT: net: Generic XDP (which introduces `xdp_prog` in `struct net_device` and
`generic_xdp_install`), after `e00c40bbd2f4` BACKPORT: net: bpf: rename ndo_xdp to ndo_bpf
(`struct netdev_bpf`), and after `19ce6ace41bb` BACKPORT: xdp: support simultaneous driver and hw XDP
attachment (`__dev_xdp_query()`, which this commit deletes/replaces with `dev_xdp_prog_id()`).

After those are merged, resolve as **MERGE**:
- Block 1: take theirs (`bpf_prog_offload_ops`, `enum bpf_xdp_mode`, `bpf_xdp_entity`,
  `struct netdev_bpf`). `struct netdev_xdp` from sdm670 can be deleted safely: the only implementers of
  `ndo_xdp` in a30605a54f3b are `drivers/net/ethernet/mellanox/mlx4/en_netdev.c` and
  `drivers/net/ethernet/mellanox/mlx5/core/en_main.c`, and both are disabled
  (`# CONFIG_MLX4_CORE is not set`, `# CONFIG_MLX5_CORE is not set`,
  arch/arm64/configs/gts4lv_eur_open_defconfig:1787-1788 @ a30605a54f3b). No Qualcomm/Samsung driver
  implements it, so no driver port is needed for this commit.
- Block 2: take theirs - both `xdp_prog` and `xdp_state[]`.
- Block 3: take theirs, and **keep** Samsung's `dev_hard_start_xmit_list()` prototype (unrelated
  adjacent declaration; net/core/dev.c defines it, dropping the prototype breaks the build).
- Blocks 4-6 (net/core/dev.c): take theirs entirely - this is the 4-arg `dev_change_xdp_fd` rework. But
  every in-tree caller of the old 2-arg `dev_change_xdp_fd(dev, fd)` must be updated at the same time
  (net/core/rtnetlink.c is the in-tree caller; Qualcomm drivers are unlikely to call it).
- Block 7 (net/core/rtnetlink.c): take theirs.

Note for the lead: this commit obsoletes `XDP_QUERY_PROG`, so `19ce6ace41bb` and this commit should be
resolved together or `19ce6ace41bb` applied first; it is pointless to hand-resolve
`__dev_xdp_query()` in isolation if this commit deletes it a few commits later.
