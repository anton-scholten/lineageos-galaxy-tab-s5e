## Proposed resolution
MERGE: keep both sides of the tcp_ipv6.c hunk, take the series side everywhere else.
- net/ipv6/tcp_ipv6.c: insert `tcp_v6_pre_connect()` (the series version) above `tcp_v6_connect()`, then keep
  sdm670's existing three-line `static int tcp_v6_connect(...)` declaration - do not reformat it. The
  `.pre_connect = tcp_v6_pre_connect,` line in `struct proto tcpv6_prot` auto-merges; check it landed.
- include/linux/bpf-cgroup.h, include/uapi/linux/bpf.h, kernel/bpf/syscall.c, net/core/filter.c: take theirs.
  If `ed5d784d9001` is resolved first (it is CLEAN, so it should be), most of these hunks reduce to just the
  INET4_CONNECT / INET6_CONNECT additions and the "ours is empty" blocks disappear.
Watch out: the new attach types must keep their uapi numbering. `BPF_CGROUP_INET4_CONNECT` /
`BPF_CGROUP_INET6_CONNECT` are explicitly numbered in enum bpf_attach_type; do not let the merge renumber
`BPF_CGROUP_INET4_BIND = 8` / `INET6_BIND = 9`, or netd's pinned attach-type numbers will not match.
