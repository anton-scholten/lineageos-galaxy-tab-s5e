include/linux/vmalloc.h's sibling style in sdm670's kernel/fork.c (lines 199-204),
but take theirs' flag removal — i.e. the result should read
`THREADINFO_GFP,` on the line that is currently `THREADINFO_GFP | __GFP_HIGHMEM,`.

would leave the call formatted differently from the rest of sdm670's
`alloc_thread_stack_node()`. Either side's flag content is a no-op on arm64, so
this is low-risk either way; the only thing that matters is that the other 15
hunks are kept, or explicit `__GFP_HIGHMEM` stays in 6 more mm/vmalloc.c sites.
