## Proposed resolution
MERGE, with one hard warning about the sched.h block.
**Do NOT take the whole "theirs" side of the sched.h hunk.** sdm670 has no
`randomized_struct_fields_start` / `randomized_struct_fields_end` macro **anywhere in the tree** -
`git grep -n 'randomized_struct_fields' a30605a54f3b` returns nothing at all, while the series base has them at
d54533f1546b:include/linux/compiler.h:477-479 and d54533f1546b:include/linux/compiler-gcc.h:293-294. No commit in
d54533f1546b..baa585f67e0e adds them (`git log -S'randomized_struct_fields_end' d54533f1546b..baa585f67e0e --
include/linux/sched.h` is empty, i.e. the usage count in sched.h never changes during the series). So if you
copy the theirs side verbatim you introduce `randomized_struct_fields_end` into include/linux/sched.h with
nothing to define it, and the build dies with exactly the first error in
analysis/build-test/port-first-errors.txt:1 - `include/linux/sched.h:2284:2: error: unknown type name
'randomized_struct_fields_end'`. That is task K4a's error and this conflict is a likely source of it.
Instead, resolve sched.h by keeping **sdm670's** side of the block and inserting only:
`#ifdef CONFIG_BPF_SYSCALL` / `struct bpf_run_ctx *bpf_ctx;` / `#endif` immediately before
`/* CPU-specific state of this task */` at a30605a54f3b:include/linux/sched.h:2207, plus the
`struct bpf_run_ctx;` forward declaration from block 2.
- include/linux/bpf.h: take theirs for blocks 3, 4 and 5. Block 5 is the real semantic change (iterate
  `_array->items[]` and wrap each program in `bpf_set_run_ctx()` / `bpf_reset_run_ctx()`); it depends on the
  prog_array conversion from earlier commit `1b238c542e86` (CLEAN).
- include/linux/bpf-cgroup.h and kernel/bpf/helpers.c: take theirs (mostly no-ops once the earlier commits
  are in). The `bpf_cgroup_storage_*()` stubs matter because this commit *deletes* the per-CPU cgroup-storage
  slot; without them, `bpf_get_local_storage()` loses its fallback.
- kernel/bpf/local_storage.c modify/delete: keep the series version (it exists from CLEAN commit
  `29249873a46a`); "ours deleted" is just sdm670 never having had it. Do not resolve this as a deletion.
Do not drop this commit: `current->bpf_ctx` is the ambient-context mechanism that cgroup and socket storage rely
on, and it is the 5.15 behaviour Android 16's netd expects.
