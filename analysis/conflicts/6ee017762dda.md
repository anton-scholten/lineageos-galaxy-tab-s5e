## Proposed resolution
MERGE - for the add/add, take **theirs** (the series version of `include/linux/refcount.h`), i.e.
resolve the file as "theirs wins". Rationale, in order of importance:
1. It is provably lossless: the two files differ only in *where* four functions live, and the series
   version is the one the rest of the series expects.
2. Keeping sdm670's inlined header instead (which is what a `DROP` or a "take ours" would do) sets up a
   **guaranteed build break** as soon as `e9c1bdddb9a0` lands: `lib/refcount.c` defines those four
   functions non-static while sdm670's header would still define them `static inline` in the same
   translation unit's include chain.
3. `lib/Kconfig.debug` needs no action at all - accept the auto-merge, which is empty.
**If you would rather keep sdm670's inline version** (defensible - it is the later upstream form and
avoids the extra `.o`), then this commit may be dropped, but you must also drop
`e9c1bdddb9a0` and `c8b2d2af17be`, and you lose `refcount_dec_and_lock_irqsave`. Do not do one without
the other two.
