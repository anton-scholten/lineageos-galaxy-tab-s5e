Unofficial **LineageOS 24 (Android 17)** for the Samsung Galaxy Tab S5e. Install guide, status and known issues: [README](https://github.com/anton-scholten/lineageos-galaxy-tab-s5e#readme).

**Coming from 24.0-20261022, 24.0-20261026, 24.0-20261105, 24.0-20261109, 24.0-20261116 or 24.0-20261120: update over the air** (*Settings → System → Updater*), no data is erased.

**What's new since 2026-11-20**
- **4K 60 fps video plays in Gallery and other apps**: the video driver refused hardware decoding above 4K 30 fps, so Gallery and other ExoPlayer-based players could not open these videos. Very high bitrate 4K 60 fps videos (H.264 above about 50 Mbit/s) still drop frames: the decoder is at its hardware limit.
- **Video thumbnails for 10-bit HEVC and AV1**: Gallery shows thumbnails for these videos again.
- **Smoother recent apps screen with many apps open**: app previews are kept at half resolution (fewer dropped frames, half the memory).
- **Rounded screen corners switch**: *Settings → Display → Other display controls → Rounded screen corners*. Applies after a restart.
- Latest LineageOS updates.

Kernel `4.9.337-ga548c1ceaed7`, device tree `b6ee69f`. Fixes owed upstream: [UPSTREAM.md](https://github.com/anton-scholten/lineageos-galaxy-tab-s5e/blob/main/UPSTREAM.md).
